      How SakuSafe works — hybrid AI, by design

      ⚡

      1. Instant scan — on-device

      Firebase AI Logic routes the first pass to Gemini Nano in Chrome. Your message never leaves your device: private, instant, and it works with no connection at all.

      2. Deep audit — cloud

      When you want the full picture, the same Firebase AI Logic SDK escalates to Gemini 2.5 Flash in the cloud for a forensic breakdown of every tactic in play.

      Scam messages contain your personal business. SakuSafe's default route keeps them on your pocket — saku means “pocket” in Indonesian — and only sends text to the cloud when you explicitly ask for the deep audit.
